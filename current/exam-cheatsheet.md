# Exam cheatsheet

### Talk About Priorities and Trade-offs (S18)
- If we release on Friday, we run the risk of breaking the app over the weekend.
- One advantage of street food is, it's cheap and local. One disadvantage is, it can be too spicy for the client.
- The main trade-off is cost versus convenience. An electric car is cheaper to run, but it takes longer to charge than filling up a petrol car.
- If we book a fancy restaurant, the client won't see the real local food.

### Compare (S17)
- Street food is the least expensive way to try local food.

### Explain an Urgent Problem (S03)
Our car is having a problem. We should have checked it before the long trip. We must find a repair shop as soon as possible. Let's try searching on Google Maps.

### Basic Presentations (S28)
Let me tell you about Hai Van Pass, one of the most beautiful roads in Vietnam.
First of all, the views are amazing: you can see the sea and the mountains from the top.
In addition, there are many good places to stop and take photos, and the road is famous on YouTube.
Of course, the road has a lot of sharp turns, and in winter it can be rainy and foggy, so we'll have to check the weather first.
To sum it up, if the weather is good, it's the best half-day trip from Da Nang.

### Demonstrating Products (S29)
So, I'd like to show you Valo. Basically, it helps organisations that manage money for people with disabilities or older people. It keeps track of all that money in one place.
The biggest problem for their staff is, they're jumping between lots of different tools that don't talk to each other. So they end up spending hours checking everything matches by hand.
With Valo, they can add what they spent, get it approved, and Valo takes it out of the person's budget right away. So they always know what's been spent and what's left.
There's also a simple approval step with the rules built in. Valo does the checking, so they save time and make fewer mistakes.
And yeah, that's it. Any questions?

### Delivering Bad News (S30)
I'm afraid I can't join the badminton tournament next week. The reason is I have to go onsite to Hanoi for work. This will affect our team, because we won't be able to play as a full team. However, I can help find someone to take my place, and I'll cheer for you online.

### Give and Receive Negative Feedback (S40)
Giving feedback
- "Hey Dung, do you have a minute?" (private)
- "Last Saturday, you arrived 30 minutes late, and it was the same the week before." (specific)
- "Because of that, we had to wait, and we lost our time on the field." (behavior + impact)
- "You're one of our best players, so we really want you there from the start." (balanced)
- "Is anything making it hard to get there on time?" (2-way)

Receiving feedback
- "Thanks for telling me." (thank)
- "Sorry, was it every week, or just the last two?" (clarify)
- "I'll leave home earlier from now on." (commit)

### Handle Complaints (S45)
- Apologize: "I'm so sorry about this."
- Acknowledge: "I understand how frustrating this is, especially when you have many expenses to submit."
- Reassure: "We'll fix this as soon as possible."
- Take ownership: "I'll investigate this issue personally."
- Dig for details: "Does it crash every time you submit, or only sometimes? What exactly happens on the screen?"
- Suggest a fix (try + -ing): "In the meantime, have you tried logging out and logging in again?"

### Missed Meeting Invites
- How many people didn't get the meeting invite?
- How upset were they?
- Was it all the sessions, or just the last one?

### Ask for More Time
- Could we push the deadline back to Friday? I need a bit more time to finish testing.
