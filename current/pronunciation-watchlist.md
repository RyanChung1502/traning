**Core diagnosis:** I know the sounds. My mouth moves on before the ending lands. It's a speed problem, not a knowledge problem. **Slowing down about 10% fixes most of it.** Accuracy collapses the moment I speed up — so lock accuracy first, speed later.

Speaking slowly is not unprofessional. Slow and clear sounds thoughtful; fast and unclear sounds nervous.

## p vs b — the hand test

Hand in front of your mouth. **P**ig = puff of air. **B**ig = no puff.
Drill: pig/big, pond/bond, pack/back.

## Daily drill (2 min)

- /z/ — how's, was, is, does, goes
- /tʃ/ — beach, teach, watch, catch, much
- final /t/ — sprint, best, want, next, it
- short /ɪ/ — sit, it, big, this, still
