# Mouth muscle exercises

Vietnamese uses a narrower jaw range and clips syllables. English needs more tongue travel. These build the range.

## Exercise 1 — Pen holding *(start here)*

1. Put a pen sideways between your teeth, biting lightly.
2. Read aloud for 2 minutes.
3. Take the pen out and read the same lines again.

**Why it works:** the pen blocks your jaw, so your tongue and lips must do all the work. When you remove it, the movement feels easy and your articulation is sharper.

**Lines to read with the pen in** — use phrases you already know, so your brain isn't busy with new words:
- "Thanks, Ryan. Next up."
- "Can you review my PR when you get a chance?"
- "Where are we on the August release?"
- "Alright, that's all the stories for this daily meeting."

## Exercise 2 — Vowel stretching

Hold each sound for **2 seconds**. Ten rounds. Keep this order.

| Sound | IPA | Mouth position |
|---|---|---|
| ah | /ɑː/ | jaw wide open |
| ee | /iː/ | lips wide, like a smile |
| oo | /uː/ | lips tight circle |
| ay | /eɪ/ | mid, then glide up |
| oh | /oʊ/ | mid, lips round |

These five are the extreme corner positions. Every other English vowel sits inside that range.
