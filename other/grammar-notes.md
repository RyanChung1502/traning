# Grammar notes

## other vs others

- **other** = adjective, always followed by a noun → "other people", "the other way"
- **others** = pronoun, stands alone → "Some agree, others don't."
- Singular → **another**

Rule: noun after it → *other*. Nothing after it → *others*.

## 's — /s/ or /z/?

The sound **before** it decides.

- After voiceless p / t / k / f / th → **/s/** → that's, it's, what's
- After vowels and voiced consonants → **/z/** → how's, where's, he's, there's

Test: put a hand on your throat. Vibration before the 's' → /z/.

## Uncountable nouns take a singular verb

No plural form → always singular verb (is / looks / has).

- *The code **looks** good* ✅ (not "look")
- *This information **is** helpful* ✅
- You can't say "two codes" / "two informations".
- "everything else" is also singular → *everything else **looks** good*.

## Note-style vs spoken shortening

- **Note-style (writing only):** drop is/are/were/the → "Comments addressed." "Build passing." "Ready for review." Good for PR comments, commits, chat status.
- **Spoken shortening:** drop the subject at the front, keep the verb → "Sounds good." "Got it." "Makes sense." Never drop the verb when speaking.
- In a meeting, put the words back: not "Comments addressed" but "The comments have been addressed."
